Motion & Brand — The Balloon

Logo, Motion & Branding

Type: Brand Identity, Motion Graphics | Spring 2026 | Tools: Adobe Illustrator, Adobe After Effects

Designed the full visual identity for The Balloon, a live studio pop culture show produced at Appalachian State University — logo, animated sting, opening title sequence, and poster reveal animations.

Poster reveal animations were built for a segment ultimately scrapped for time, using noise, texture, and worn/glitched edges for a tactile, analog feel.

My Role: Design and animation across all brand and motion elements.
