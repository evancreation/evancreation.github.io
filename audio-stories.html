Skin Deep

Type: Audio Drama | Duration: 3:33 | Fall 2025

A speculative audio drama exploring disability, autonomy, and class through the lens of biomedical technology. When Dice's prosthetic arm malfunctions, their well-meaning roommates offer to help—but the cost of accepting assistance isn't always what it seems.

Set in a near-future world where biomedical enhancements are commonplace but unequally accessible, the story examines how privilege shapes our understanding of help. Through three roommates—Dice (a biomedical student with a malfunctioning prosthetic), Audrina (a wealthy interior designer), and Duman (a working-class Southern transplant)—the drama unpacks the invisible strings attached to technological solutions and the ways accessibility becomes a luxury rather than a right.

Production: Wrote original script, directed voice actors, and mixed audio using Adobe Audition. Focused on minimal sound design to keep the emphasis on dialogue and character dynamics while using subtle audio cues to signal Dice's prosthetic malfunction.

Clothes That Make Us

Type: Audio Documentary | Duration: 1:10 | Fall 2025

An audio documentary exploring how clothing shapes identity and self-expression. Through intimate interviews, participants reflect on their favorite articles of clothing and what they reveal about personal history, confidence, and belonging.

The piece weaves together diverse perspectives—from platform Demonia boots that make someone feel tall and confident, to cargo pants that signal adulthood and professionalism, to college night shirts that carry memories of dorm life and friendship. Each story reveals how we use clothing to navigate identity, comfort, and self-presentation.

Production: Conducted interviews, edited and mixed audio using Adobe Audition. Crafted narrative structure to highlight themes of gender expression, memory, and the everyday ways clothing tells our stories.

ADR & Vocal Processing

Type: Sound Design | Fall 2025

Individual contribution to a narrative short: recorded and processed original dialogue, using pitch-shifting, layering and reverb to build a haunting, disembodied vocal quality.

Production: Recorded ADR lines and edited using Adobe Audition to create tension through sound alone.
