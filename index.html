About

Double-majoring in Electronic Media/Broadcasting and Gender, Women’s & Sexuality Studies put me in a unique position: telling the stories of the underprivileged with technical skill. These two fields look completely different on paper, yet they fit together easily in the same person.

Using what I’ve learned in my media major, I’m one of many people helping preserve the knowledge of queer communities around the world. Things like Stonewall, the AIDS Memorial Quilt, and the lives of queer public figures are remembered because people took pictures, filmed, wrote things down, and built places for those stories to live on.

That’s the work I dedicate myself to: using storytelling and technical craft to spread the stories of the small, the overlooked, the underprivileged.
